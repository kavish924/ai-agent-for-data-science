Reproducibility for Titanic and Housing: cleaned CSV, charts (9 and 11), model scores and the
in-session report re-render were all identical. The cross-session report comparison is n/a,
because live narration is stored per session.

What we learned:
- Run 1 didn't record *why* an attempt failed (the harness read the wrong `llm_calls` column;
  fixed). A re-run of Housing and Telco straight afterwards failed on all six attempts with
  `transport:LLMError`, and a direct probe then succeeded, so the failures were transient
  provider errors, not invalid plans.
- The planner retried with no wait between attempts (Telco's run 1 attempts took 59.7 s, which
  hit the 60 s timeout, then 4.0 s and 0.5 s). This led to **FR-PL-18 and FR-RP-17**: exponential
  backoff between transport retries, plus the HTTP status in `llm_calls.error`.
- The two plans that came back were Gate-valid and matched the golden expectations.
